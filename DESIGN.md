# Recipe Explorer — Design Direction

Recipe Explorer is a mise-en-place library built as a warm kitchen ledger:
cream paper, clay marks, sage state, and a buttery prep note. Ingredients and
steps sit in the same working field so the interface feels like a recipe card
that can actually be used at the counter.

The dish index, ingredient ledger, prep sequence, and pantry swap are the
product surface. Recipes and estimates are synthetic; the swap is a local
planning aid, not medical nutrition or grocery data.
